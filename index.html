01About

Hi, I'm Riley.

At Exotic Online Advertising I built the IT and engineering function from nothing: the custom platforms, the payment reconciliation, the servers they run on, the office network and support for 38 people. I also run Eversatile Solutions, where I build websites, hosting and SaaS tools for Kenyan businesses.

I studied geology at the University of Nairobi, started out repairing computers, and came into software through eMobilis and ALX. Because I support everything I ship, I build for the person who has to operate it at 2am.

Own it end to end. Requirements, code, deployment, monitoring and the support ticket after.
Boring where it counts. Queues, retries, audit trails and backups before features.
Write it down. My systems ship with READMEs, runbooks and a security model.
